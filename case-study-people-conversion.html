People Inc. · Demand Gen + Conversion

Rewriting a webinar journey to increase conversion 29%

I worked with Design and Editorial to improve the messaging and experience of a B2B webinar landing page. We focused on making the value of the event easier to understand and giving visitors a clearer path to register.

+29% funnel conversion
540+ new prospects

01 · the challenge

Making the value of the webinar easier to see

The landing page had to convince prospective customers that the event was worth their time before asking them to register. We saw an opportunity to make that decision easier by sharpening the messaging, improving the page hierarchy, and emphasizing what attendees would actually get from the experience.

02 · my role

Bringing audience insight into the page experience

I worked alongside Design and Editorial on the headline, supporting copy, CTA language, and overall page narrative. I also used audience, search, and competitive research to help shape the messaging around what prospective attendees were most likely to care about.

03 · the landing page

Giving the page a clearer story

We structured the page around the questions a visitor needed answered before registering. What is the event? Why is it relevant? What will I get from attending? What should I do next?

04 · what changed

A sharper message and an easier path to register

The refreshed page put more emphasis on the headline, value proposition, supporting copy, and registration flow. The goal was to make the reason to attend more immediate and reduce the amount of interpretation required from the visitor.

05 · impact

Clearer messaging helped improve the funnel

After the changes, funnel conversion increased by 29%, helping the program generate more than 540 new prospects.

It was one of my clearest early examples of how copy and information hierarchy can influence measurable customer behavior.

06 · what I learned

Clarity can be a growth lever

This project changed how I think about conversion work. Sometimes the biggest opportunity is simply making the value of something easier to understand.
