01 · the challenge

Turning campaign performance into a story customers could use

The campaign had strong results, but metrics alone did not explain why D/Cipher mattered. The product-marketing challenge was to turn performance into customer proof: a story that connected the advertiser’s business problem, D/Cipher’s intent-based audience strategy, and the measurable outcome in a way a buyer could understand quickly.

That meant deciding which numbers were actually persuasive, what context they needed, and how to show the product’s value without turning the story into a dense performance report.

02 · my role

Shaping performance into a clearer narrative

I supported the development of the customer story by shaping campaign performance and audience insights into concise, customer-facing messaging. I helped organize the narrative around four questions a prospective customer would care about: what problem the advertiser was solving, which audiences D/Cipher identified, how the product reached them, and what changed as a result.

That structure turned a set of media metrics into a reusable proof point for the product and gave GTM teams a cleaner way to explain D/Cipher’s value in customer conversations and enablement materials.

03 · the story

From business challenge to measurable proof

The story followed a deliberate business-to-product arc. A national jewelry brand wanted to broaden perceptions of its assortment and drive purchases ahead of Valentine’s Day. D/Cipher used gifting, apparel, fashion-jewelry, and fashion-trend intent segments to reach consumers whose behavior indicated relevance across the premium open web.

The results became the proof layer of the positioning: a 7.5-point lift in brand awareness, a 5.1-point lift in purchase intent, and a 6-point lift in incremental performance across the funnel. Presenting those outcomes together helped show that the value extended beyond a single click metric.

+6 PPTS incremental performance across the funnel
+7.5 PPTS brand awareness
+5.1 PPTS purchase intent

04 · the insight

Finding the story behind the numbers

The performance data also gave us a second layer of product insight: where the audience was most responsive. Holiday gift reviews drove a 153% CTR lift, fashion-trend content drove a 60% lift, and awards-show content saw a 17% boost.

Instead of treating those numbers as isolated optimizations, the story connected them back to D/Cipher’s core value proposition. Intent signals were not only helping find an audience; they were revealing the content contexts and consumer mindsets most likely to drive engagement.

05 · why it mattered

Making the product value easier to see

The finished story made D/Cipher’s value more concrete by tying the product directly to a customer challenge, an audience strategy, and measurable outcomes. That matters in product marketing because customer evidence gives buyers a reason to believe the positioning, not just understand it.

It also created a compact asset that could support broader GTM and sales-enablement work: a real example of the product finding relevant audiences, surfacing useful insights, and driving movement across the funnel.

06 · what I learned

Data is more useful when it tells a story

This project reinforced that the strongest customer stories do more than celebrate a result. They explain the mechanism behind it. For product marketing, that means choosing proof points that reinforce the value proposition and giving the audience enough context to understand why the outcome is meaningful.
