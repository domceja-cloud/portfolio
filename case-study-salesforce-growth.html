01 · the challenge

Making CRM feel more approachable for small-businesses

Starter Suite was designed to give small businesses an easier entry point into Salesforce. The challenge was making CRM feel clear and relevant to business owners who were not necessarily thinking in traditional enterprise-software terms.

We wanted the campaign to feel native to social while still communicating the product clearly enough to move people toward trial and adoption.

02 · my role

From product positioning to campaign execution

I helped translate Starter Suite positioning into creator briefs, social concepts, and campaign messaging. I coordinated two agencies and five creators through review and approval cycles and worked with Growth, Paid Media, Legal, Brand, and Localization to bring the work live.

I also built and QA’d CTA links and UTM tracking, monitored performance, and supported post-launch reporting so we could connect engagement back to product interest and conversion.

03 · the work

Social-first product storytelling

The campaign focused on recognizable small-business pain points instead of leading with product terminology. Creator content gave us a way to show the product through people, scenarios, and formats that already felt familiar on TikTok, Meta, and YouTube.

04 · how I approached it

Starting with the audience

I used social and audience research to understand the language, pain points, and hooks that felt most relevant to small-business audiences. Those insights helped shape creator briefs, scripts, and campaign messaging.

During production, I kept feedback moving across internal teams and external partners while helping ensure the work stayed aligned to the product story and Salesforce brand.

05 · impact

Reach was only part of the story

The campaign generated more than 2M views and contributed to 100+ prospective SMB customers. Trial-to-paid conversion also increased by 24%, giving us a stronger signal that the campaign was influencing behavior further down the funnel.

06 · what I learned

Stories work best when audiences see themselves in them

The strongest content connected product value to a problem the audience already recognized. That lesson has stayed with me in later messaging and GTM work.
