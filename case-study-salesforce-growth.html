Salesforce · Product Marketing + Creator Growth

Taking Starter Suite from positioning to a 24% lift in trial-to-paid conversion

I helped bring Starter Suite positioning into a cross-platform creator campaign built for small-business audiences. The work spanned messaging, creator execution, and campaign measurement, and ultimately contributed to 2M+ views, 100+ prospective customers, and a 24% lift in trial-to-paid conversion.

01 · the challenge

Making CRM feel more approachable for small-business audiences

Starter Suite was designed to give small businesses an easier way into Salesforce. The challenge was making the value of CRM feel clear and relevant to newer business owners who were not necessarily thinking in traditional enterprise-software terms.

We wanted the campaign to meet people where they already were, using social-first content that felt natural on TikTok, Meta, and YouTube while still communicating the product clearly.

02 · my role

From product positioning to campaign execution

I worked across messaging, creator strategy, campaign operations, and performance reporting. I helped translate Starter Suite positioning into creator briefs and social concepts, coordinated two agencies and five creators through review and approval cycles, and partnered with Growth, Paid Media, Legal, Brand, and Localization teams to bring the campaign live.

I also supported campaign measurement through CTA and UTM tracking, reporting, and post-launch analysis so we could see how social engagement translated into product interest and conversion.

03 · the work

Social-first product storytelling

A big part of the creative strategy was grounding the product in familiar small-business pain points instead of leading with CRM language. The content focused on the problems business owners were already trying to solve and showed where Salesforce could fit.

Creator-led content helped the product feel more natural in-feed and gave us a way to communicate benefits through people, scenarios, and formats that audiences already understood.

04 · how I approached it

Starting with the audience

I used social and audience research to understand the language, pain points, and hooks that felt most relevant to small-business audiences. Those insights helped shape creator briefs, scripts, and campaign messaging.

During production, I coordinated feedback across internal teams and external partners, helped keep assets aligned to brand and product messaging, and supported amplification and measurement once the work went live.

05 · impact

Reach was only part of the story

The campaign generated more than 2M views across social channels and contributed to 100+ prospective SMB customers.

The stronger signal was downstream. Trial-to-paid conversion increased by 24%, which showed that the campaign was doing more than building awareness. It was helping move audiences further into the product journey.

06 · what I learned

Product stories work best when the audience can see themselves in them

This project reinforced how much good product marketing depends on audience understanding. The strongest content connected product value to a problem the audience already recognized.

That lesson has stayed with me. Clear positioning matters, but it becomes much more useful when it feels grounded in the way customers actually think and talk.
