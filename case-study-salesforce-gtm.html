Salesforce · Messaging + GTM

Bringing Agentforce and Starter Suite stories to market

I worked across product messaging, web copy, launch content, and sales enablement for Salesforce products including Agentforce and Starter Suite. A lot of the work came down to taking technical capabilities and turning them into language customers and internal teams could use more easily.

01 · the challenge

Making technical products easier to understand

Agentforce and Starter Suite each came with a different messaging challenge. Both had strong product stories, but those stories still had to be translated into language that made sense to the audiences seeing them for the first time.

I spent a lot of my time working in that middle layer between product priorities and customer-facing communication, helping shape how features showed up across web, campaign, social, and enablement materials.

02 · my role

Connecting product, message, and launch

I developed and refined launch materials across Salesforce.com product pages, product walkthroughs, creator scripts, launch recaps, campaign briefs, and sales enablement.

That meant working closely with Product Marketing, Growth, Web, Legal, Brand, Localization, Paid Media, and Sales so the messaging stayed accurate and consistent as it moved from one channel to another.

03 · product messaging

Turning internal product language into customer-facing copy

For web and launch content, I focused on making product value easier to understand quickly. I worked on headlines, body copy, CTAs, and supporting messaging, with an emphasis on tying features back to customer needs and use cases.

04 · product messaging

Making Agentforce feel concrete

Agentforce brought a different kind of challenge because the product sat in a fast-moving AI category. The goal was to make the technology feel useful and specific without flattening what made the product interesting.

I supported customer-facing messaging and launch materials that connected technical capabilities to practical business outcomes and clearer reasons to care.

05 · launch enablement

Giving teams a consistent story to work from

The customer story also had to work internally. I developed launch recaps, product walkthroughs, campaign briefs, and sales-facing materials that helped teams understand key capabilities, use cases, and campaign priorities.

Those materials helped connect product messaging to execution across Sales, Marketing, and partner teams.

06 · cross-functional execution

Keeping the story consistent across teams

Getting the work live involved a lot of coordination across Product, Growth, Web Experience, Brand, Legal, Localization, agencies, creators, and Paid Media.

I helped manage those handoffs and reviews while keeping the messaging tied back to the original product story.

07 · what I learned

Clarity shapes how people experience the product

This work made me more interested in the communication side of product marketing. The way a product is explained has a real effect on whether customers understand it, whether teams can sell it, and whether people can picture themselves using it.

That translation from product complexity to customer clarity is one of the parts of product marketing I want to keep developing.
